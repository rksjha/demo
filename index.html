One verified identity across priority sectors

The public site is designed to feed a future portal where profiles, documents, invoices, reward rules and redemptions can be governed by client type, sector and compliance status.

Sector Reward use case Portal readiness
MSMEs Service credits for diagnostics, funding readiness and compliance. Business profile, GST/PAN, document checklist.
Farmers and dealers Agri input loyalty, dealer incentives and seasonal campaigns. Registration, region, product movement and settlement rules.
Enterprises Project-level advisory rewards and cross-department allocations. Organization roles, invoice mapping and finance reports.
Partners Offer acceptance, redemption validation and controlled settlement. Partner agreement, offer catalog and dispute workflow.

How SHIVACOIN works

A simple lifecycle from participation to future utility review.

  1. 1

    Create profile

    User, organization, consent and documentation are captured.

  2. 2

    Confirm eligible spend

    Invoice and payment events pass policy and duplicate checks.

  3. 3

    Issue coins

    Rewards move from pending to active after settlement controls.

  4. 4

    Redeem services

    Coins apply to approved SCG service, training or partner utility.

  5. 5

    Review future utility

    Transfer, encashment and tokenization require legal clearance.
