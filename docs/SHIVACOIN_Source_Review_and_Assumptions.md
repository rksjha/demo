## Important Assumptions

- SHIVACOIN should start as a loyalty reward, not a public investment product.
- The Rs 1 per Rs 1,000 spend rule is treated as a base reward value.
- Future 20X-100X upside is treated only as an aspirational scenario.
- Trading, transfer and encashment require legal, tax and AML review before activation.
- The first website should include white paper, reward calculator, onboarding CTA, inquiry capture and risk disclosure.
- The first portal should include profile registration, reward ledger and admin approval workflows.
