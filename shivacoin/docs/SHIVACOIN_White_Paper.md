# SHIVACOIN White Paper

Version 0.1  
Date: 12 May 2026  
Status: Planning draft, not an investment offer

## Abstract

SHIVACOIN is proposed as a reward and utility layer for the SCG ecosystem, designed to convert client engagement, verified spending, referrals and partner participation into a transparent digital reward balance. The platform begins as a closed-loop loyalty system and may evolve toward controlled transferability, partner redemption and regulated tokenization if business adoption and compliance readiness support that path.

The founding reward principle is simple: for every Rs 1,000 of eligible spend, a client earns SHIVACOIN worth Rs 1 at issuance. Future market value is not guaranteed. Any upside depends on real adoption, platform utility, partner demand, liquidity, regulation and governance.

## Problem

SCG-family businesses serve multiple audiences: MSMEs, advisory clients, farmers, dealers, distributors, mentees and partners. These users interact across separate websites, services, documents, invoices and relationship channels. Without a unified rewards and identity layer, the ecosystem loses opportunities to:

- Encourage repeat engagement.
- Capture client profiles and documentation readiness.
- Reward referrals and partner activity.
- Create measurable loyalty across sectors.
- Build a future-ready digital transaction layer.
- Convert service relationships into long-term ecosystem participation.

## Proposed Solution

SHIVACOIN introduces:

- A verified client profile.
- A reward ledger linked to eligible spend.
- Service and partner redemption.
- Transparent transaction history.
- Admin-governed reward rules.
- Compliance-first controls before transfer or encashment.
- Future tokenization readiness.

The platform should be built around business utility rather than speculative trading. A client should first see SHIVACOIN as a useful service credit, benefit, loyalty asset and ecosystem passport.

## Utility

Initial utility:

- Redeem against SCG advisory services.
- Redeem for diagnostics, documentation support, training and subscriptions.
- Apply coins as service credits for future invoices.
- Access member-only offers.
- Participate in referral campaigns.

Extended utility:

- Partner merchant offers.
- Dealer and distributor incentives.
- Farmer loyalty programs.
- Training and certification credits.
- Documentation readiness packages.
- Priority support and premium advisory access.

Future utility, subject to compliance:

- Verified user-to-user transfer.
- Encashment requests.
- Tokenized wallet representation.
- Partner settlement rails.
- External marketplace integrations.

## Reward Issuance

Base policy:

```text
Rs 1,000 eligible spend = SHIVACOIN worth Rs 1 at issuance
```

Eligibility should depend on:

- Verified profile.
- Valid invoice.
- Realized payment.
- No refund or chargeback.
- Product/service category eligibility.
- Fraud and duplicate checks.

## Governance

The platform requires a formal governance model:

- Reward Policy Committee: approves issuance rules and campaigns.
- Compliance Reviewer: reviews KYC, AML, VDA, tax and advertising risks.
- Finance Controller: manages reward liability and settlement reserves.
- Technology Owner: manages security, uptime, audit logs and integrations.
- Partner Manager: approves partner acceptance and settlement.

## Supply and Liability

SHIVACOIN should not start with a speculative fixed supply promise. For the loyalty phase, coins should be minted or posted only against eligible business activity, campaign incentives or approved administrative adjustments.

Recommended controls:

- Every issued coin links to an event: invoice, campaign, referral, correction or migration.
- Every reversal links to a refund, chargeback, expiry or policy breach.
- Every redemption links to a service, partner settlement or approved encashment case.
- Treasury reports reconcile issued, pending, active, redeemed, expired and reversed balances.

## Technology Model

MVP:

- Web application.
- PostgreSQL ledger database.
- Admin console.
- Client portal.
- Audit log.
- CSV/manual invoice ingestion.
- Email/SMS notifications.

Scale stage:

- API integrations with CRM, accounting, payment gateway and partner systems.
- Rule engine.
- KYC provider.
- Fraud monitoring.
- Data warehouse.

Tokenization stage:

- Smart contract only after legal review.
- Custodial or non-custodial wallet decision.
- Bridge between internal ledger and on-chain supply.
- Independent security audit.
- AML/CFT monitoring.

## Risk Disclosures

SHIVACOIN must not be marketed as a guaranteed investment. Possible future appreciation of 20X-100X is only a scenario and may never occur. Users may lose utility value due to expiry, regulatory restrictions, business policy changes, lack of liquidity, account breach, fraud findings or market non-adoption.

Before enabling trading, transfer or encashment, SCG should obtain professional legal, tax and compliance advice.

| 2 | Automation | Invoice/payment integration, KYC, reports |
| 3 | Partner utility | Marketplace, dealer/farmer/service credits |

## Conclusion

SHIVACOIN can become a unified reward, loyalty and ecosystem participation layer for SCG. The right strategic posture is not to start with a speculative token, but to build a trusted business utility system that earns adoption first and only later expands toward regulated token economics.
