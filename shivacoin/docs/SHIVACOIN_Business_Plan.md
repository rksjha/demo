# SHIVACOIN Business Plan

Date: 12 May 2026

## 1. Business Summary

SHIVACOIN is a loyalty, utility and future token-readiness platform for the SCG ecosystem. It rewards verified client spending and participation with digital coins that can be redeemed for services, partner benefits and, subject to compliance, future transfer or encashment.

The business should be positioned as a trust-building engagement system, not as a speculative crypto launch.

## 2. Strategic Objectives

- Increase repeat purchases across SCG services.
- Capture verified client profiles and business documents.
- Create a unified rewards identity across MSME, consulting, agri, mentorship and partner channels.
- Improve lead conversion through reward-linked onboarding.
- Build partner marketplace utility.
- Prepare a compliant foundation for future tokenization if commercially justified.

## 3. Market Segments

| Segment | Offer | Reward use case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MSMEs | Diagnostics, compliance, funding readiness, consulting | Earn on service spend; redeem for documentation and advisory credits |
| Startups | Mentorship, strategy, investor readiness | Earn through subscriptions and service packages |
| Farmers | Agri products and advisory | Earn on purchases; redeem for inputs/training |
| Dealers | Distribution volume and schemes | Earn channel incentives and redeem against invoices |
| Enterprises | Transformation and governance advisory | Earn project-level credits |
| Partners | Merchant and service network | Accept coins and receive settlement |

## 4. Revenue Model

Direct revenue:

- Consulting and service package sales.
- Premium membership plans.
- Documentation readiness packages.
- Training, certification and advisory subscriptions.
- Partner onboarding and listing fees.

Indirect revenue:

- Higher client retention.
- Increased purchase frequency.
- Lower lead acquisition cost through referrals.
- Cross-selling across SCG businesses.
- Data-driven service targeting.

Future revenue, subject to compliance:

- Marketplace transaction fees.
- Partner settlement fee.
- Encashment processing fee.
- Token ecosystem service fee.
- API access for partners.

## 5. Cost Model

| Cost head | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Reward liability | Coins issued against eligible spend |
| Platform development | Website, portal, backend, admin, integrations |
| Compliance | Legal opinions, tax review, AML/KYC setup |
| Operations | Support, KYC review, partner management |
| Marketing | Launch campaigns, content, events |
| Security | Audit, monitoring, penetration testing |
| Settlement reserve | Partner redemption and encashment coverage |

## 6. Unit Economics

Base reward cost:

- Rs 1 per Rs 1,000 eligible spend.
- Base cost is 0.10% before redemption behavior and expiry.

Example:

| Eligible platform spend | Base rewards issued | Gross reward cost |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rs 10,00,000 | 1,000 coins | Rs 1,000 |
| Rs 1,00,00,000 | 10,000 coins | Rs 10,000 |
| Rs 10,00,00,000 | 1,00,000 coins | Rs 1,00,000 |

This is commercially manageable if rewards primarily drive repeat purchase, service redemption, referrals and partner-funded offers.

## 9. Risk Register

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investment-like marketing | Regulatory and trust risk | Use loyalty and utility language; avoid guaranteed upside |
| Fraudulent invoices | Financial loss | Payment verification, duplicate detection, admin review |
| Reward liability growth | Balance sheet pressure | Reserve policy, expiry, redemption limits |
| KYC data mishandling | Privacy and legal risk | Access controls, encryption, consent management |
| Encashment complexity | Tax/payment/AML risk | Delay until legal clearance and controlled limits |
| Low utility | Poor adoption | Build service credits and partner catalog early |
| Partner settlement disputes | Operational friction | Clear agreements and settlement reports |
| Blockchain premature launch | Technical and compliance risk | Keep tokenization as Phase 5 |

## 10. Financial Planning Assumptions

Conservative model:

- Reward cost remains 0.10% of eligible spend.
- Redemption is mostly service credit.
- Partner-funded offers reduce SCG liability.
- Encashment is capped and approved manually.

Growth model:

- Membership plans and repeat services improve lifetime value.
- Dealer and farmer campaigns add volume.
- Partner marketplace increases redemption utility.
- Data insights improve cross-selling.

## 12. Board-Level Recommendation

Proceed with SHIVACOIN as a controlled rewards and utility platform. Do not launch it as a tradeable investment token at the beginning. The strongest business path is to build trusted utility, gather verified users, create redemption demand, and preserve a future regulated token path only after adoption and legal readiness are proven.
